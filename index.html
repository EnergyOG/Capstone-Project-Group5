Our Story

About Lumière Beauty

Born from a love of timeless femininity and modern artistry, Lumière Beauty celebrates the romance of the Rose Quartz Evening palette — rich maroon elegance paired with soft blush warmth.

Crafted with passion since 2018

What began as a small studio in New York's Beauty District has grown into a beloved destination for brides, creatives, and everyday women who want to feel extraordinary. Our artists blend technique with intuition — every brushstroke designed to enhance, never mask, your natural beauty.

We source only cruelty-free, premium formulas and curate each product in our boutique with the same care we bring to every client appointment.
